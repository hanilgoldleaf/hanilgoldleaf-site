Products

Gold Leaf · Flakes · Powder

From thin sheets of gold leaf to torn gold flakes and fine gold powder, choose the form and size that suits your work.

Gold Leaf 金箔

Gold beaten into thin single sheets. We make it in several sizes: the regular 11 × 11 cm sheet for gilding art, crafts, exteriors, interiors and historic buildings, dancheong leaf for outdoor work, 4-cut and 6-cut (the regular sheet cut into 4 or 6) for coating herbal pills (gongjindan), and large sheets for plaques and cards.

  • Art · Crafts
  • Dancheong
  • Pharmaceutical
  • Plaques · Cards

Gold Flakes 金屑

Gold leaf torn into small flakes. Widely used to decorate food such as cakes and as a cosmetic ingredient. Flake size is 2~16 mesh. The minimum pack is 2g, and you can add 1g at a time (e.g. 2g, 3g, 4g).

  • Food decoration
  • Cosmetics

Gold Powder 金粉

Gold leaf ground as fine as flour. Packed in 2g bags and used for calligraphy, painting, cosmetics and craft finishing.

  • Calligraphy · Painting
  • Cosmetics
  • Craft finishing

Applications

Wherever gold is needed

We hold a pharmaceutical manufacturing license and herbal medicine GMP certification, and we put purity and safety first for edible gold.

  • Pharmaceutical 4-cut · 6-cut

    Gold leaf coats herbal pills such as gongjindan. The regular 11 × 11 cm sheet is cut into 4 or 6 pieces (4-cut, 6-cut) to match the pill size.

  • Food additive Flakes · Leaf

    Edible gold to garnish fine dishes, drinks and cakes.

  • Cosmetics Flakes · Powder

    Gold flakes and powder as an ingredient for creams, essences and more.

  • Art · Crafts · Exterior · Interior · Dancheong Gilding · Dancheong

    Regular 11 × 11 cm leaf for gilding artworks, crafts, exterior and interior surfaces, and for historic building restoration. Dancheong leaf is pressed onto paper, so it stays put in the wind during outdoor work.

  • Keum-boo Gold Export

    Gold sheets for keum-boo, the technique of fusing gold onto silver. Available in 1g and 2g sheets and 1g and 2g half sizes, 3 sheets per booklet. Currently exported to the United States.

  • Plaques · Cards Card

    Large sheets sized for plaques and cards.
